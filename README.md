## 🌍 What is Airwave?

Airwave brings **63,168+ radio stations** into one listening space. Find something familiar, explore somewhere new, or let Airwave pick a station for you.

Search by station name, country, language, or genre. Save favourites, revisit recently played stations, and keep listening while you browse.

**No account. No installation. Just open Airwave and tune in.**

## 🎧 How to use

1. **[Open Airwave](https://prasannaone.github.io/Airwave/)** in your browser.
2. Search for a station or browse the catalogue.
3. Narrow things down with the country, language, or genre filters.
4. Select a station and start listening.
5. Save favourites, revisit recently played stations, or try **Surprise Me**.

An internet connection is required to load the station catalogue and play live streams. Use an up-to-date browser.

## 💾 Your data

Airwave saves favourites, recently played stations, hidden stations, and browsing preferences in your browser when local storage is available.

- Your data stays in that browser and is **not automatically synced between devices**.
- JSON import and export cover **favourites only**.
- Importing a backup merges favourites without duplicates.
- Imported stations that are not in the catalogue are kept separately and remain playable.
- Hidden stations, listening history, and browsing preferences are not included in the favourites backup.

## ⚙️ Built with

- **HTML**
- **CSS**
- **Vanilla JavaScript**
- Browser audio playback and local storage

Airwave is a static, client-side web app. The station directory is based on [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/), a community-driven radio station directory.

Station streams are provided by the stations themselves, so availability and playback quality may vary.
