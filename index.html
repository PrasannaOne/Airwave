About Airwave

The world on air, gathered in one place.

Airwave.

Find your frequency. Your lil corner of radio. Airwave brings internet radio stations from around the world into one simple listening space.

Getting started

Browse the station catalogue or use search to find a station by name. Choose a station to start listening. The mini-player stays available while you explore.

Find your stations

  • Search: Look up stations by name or available catalogue details.
  • Filters: Narrow results by country, language or genre. The letter picker helps you jump through long lists.
  • Surprise Me: Pick a random playable station from your current results.

Make it yours

  • Favourites: Tap the heart to save a station.
  • Hide: Remove stations you don't want to see. Restore them later in Settings → Hidden stations.
  • Recent: Return to stations you've listened to.
  • Player: Tap or swipe up to expand. Swipe down or use the close control to collapse.
  • Sleep timer: Choose 5–60 minutes to stop playback automatically.

Back up your stations

Export a JSON backup of your favourites and hidden stations. Import it later to merge those saved stations into another Airwave browser session. The bundled catalogue is not changed.

Credits

The station catalogue is powered by Radio Browser. Airwave is an independent project and is not affiliated with the listed stations.
